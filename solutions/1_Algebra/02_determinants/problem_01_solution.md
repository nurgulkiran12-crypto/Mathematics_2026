Question
Compute the determinants of the following two matrices:
A = [3 -2; 5 4] and B = [1 7; 2 14]
Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

Solution
Computing the determinants:

For matrix A = [3 -2; 5 4]:
det(A) = (3)(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 - (-10) = 22

For matrix B = [1 7; 2 14]:
det(B) = (1)(14) - (7)(2) = 14 - 14 = 0

Invertibility and Justification:

Matrix A ([3 -2; 5 4]) is invertible because its determinant is 22, which is non-zero (det != 0).

Matrix B ([1 7; 2 14]) is not invertible because its determinant is 0.
